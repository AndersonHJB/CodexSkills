```text
Lighting/mood: bright, confident, warm.
Color palette: warm white, coral red, teal green, charcoal black, small yellow accents; balanced palette, no dominant blue/purple gradient.
Text (verbatim, render exactly in Simplified Chinese, no extra text):
"[Line 1]"
"[Line 2]"
"[Line 3]"
"[Line 4]"
"[Line 5]"
Typography: large clean bold Simplified Chinese sans-serif, high contrast, all text perfectly spelled.
Constraints: Only include the verbatim Chinese text above. Do not add QR codes, phone numbers, watermarks, English filler, lorem ipsum, or misspelled characters. Keep all text inside the image.
```
