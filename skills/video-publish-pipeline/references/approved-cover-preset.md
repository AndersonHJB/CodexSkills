# Approved cover preset

This is the fixed default cover system for `house-creative` mode. It is derived from the approved New Zealand SIM tutorial cover set, but only its visual DNA and layout logic are reusable. Every title, brand, screenshot, step, disclosure, and claim must come from the current video's fact matrix and source frames. A user-named different style reference overrides this preset for that run; otherwise do not drift from it.

## Fixed visual DNA

| Role | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Background | warm milk-white paper, `#FEFCF6` / `#FAF6EB`, with restrained fiber, grain, and layered-paper edges |
| Primary ink | deep navy `#1A1A2E`, used for the dominant Chinese hook |
| Main accents | warm yellow `#F4D758` and cobalt/deep blue `#2B7FD8` / `#1E5BA8` |
| Minor accent | red `#E84A5F`, only for a small warning or hand-drawn mark |
| Source colors | preserve the real product/UI/brand colors inside evidence panels; do not recolor them into the decoration palette |
| Main title | heavyweight, angular editorial Chinese display lettering; dense spacing, high contrast, optional cream sticker edge and restrained hard shadow |
| Supporting text | Alibaba PuHuiTi 2.0 SemiBold or a metric-compatible local fallback with verified Chinese glyphs |
| Materials | yellow marker underline, blue brush band or hand-drawn frame, torn-paper note, tape, arrow, check, or short radiating strokes |

Use only two or three material motifs on one cover. Keep the editorial paper-collage look; reject neon, cyan or blue-purple gradients, glassmorphism, glossy 3D, generic dark-tech styling, stock-photo decoration, and uniform rounded-card dashboards.

## Content hierarchy

Every cover follows this reading order:

1. one dominant Chinese hook;
2. one object, audience, or scope label;
3. one or two pieces of real visual proof;
4. a small creator trust anchor when the source contains a real person;
5. at most one necessary disclosure or badge.

Derive these slots from the current fact matrix: `HOOK`, `ENTITY`, `PROOF_A`, optional `PROOF_B`, optional `VERIFIED_STEPS`, and optional `DISCLOSURE`. Never reuse topic-specific copy such as an earlier product, brand, location, price, or result.

Use a real source frame for the creator portrait, usually as a small circle or light torn-edge cutout with a thick cream border and restrained shadow. Do not generate a new face or let the portrait dominate the hook. Evidence panels must remain recognizable source UI, product, device, chart, or result imagery rather than invented proof.

## Deterministic layout selection

- One subject plus one proof point: use a single large evidence card.
- One subject plus two or three verified steps: use the portrait step-poster structure.
- Two subjects, two products, or before/after evidence: use a balanced dual-evidence structure.
- The same fact structure should select the same layout family. Do not randomize font family, palette, portrait position, or the number of evidence panels.

## 16:9 — strong hook with stacked evidence

- Reserve roughly 42% of the width for the hook and 58% for evidence.
- Put a one- or two-line oversized title at upper-left, a blue brush object label below it, and at most one small yellow badge near lower-left.
- Stack two real evidence cards on the right with a deliberate offset and light overlap. Place the small creator portrait near the lower-right evidence edge when available.
- Use yellow marker strokes to bridge empty space and allow decorative blue brush texture to bleed at the lower edge.
- Keep title, face, proof, and badge inside a 6%–8% semantic safe area; only non-semantic decoration may touch the frame.

## 3:4 — vertical tutorial poster

- Use the upper 22%–27% for a tilted yellow eyebrow, dominant title, yellow underline, and blue hand-drawn entity frame.
- Use the lower 65%–72% for a vertical step spine plus evidence stack. Never crop the 16:9 design into this ratio.
- When there are two or three short verified steps, place torn-paper step notes in the left 24%–28% and corresponding real evidence panels in the right 72%–76%. This is the preferred richer layout.
- When concise step copy is not supported, use large numbers and arrows only, with wider stacked evidence panels. Do not invent explanatory sentences to fill space.
- Keep the creator portrait near the upper-right edge of the evidence region without covering the title or critical fields.
- Use a bottom yellow tape note only for a necessary, verified limitation or disclosure. Otherwise leave the space quiet.

## 4:3 — balanced comparison board

- Use the upper 20%–24% for a centered headline and one deep-blue pill label.
- Place two equal-weight real evidence panels side by side. If the content has only one subject, pair overview/detail or entry/key-step evidence; never duplicate an image to fake comparison.
- Place a small creator portrait at the lower seam between panels, occupying no more than about 17%–20% of the short edge.
- Place an optional yellow disclosure badge below the portrait with at least 6%–8% bottom safe area.
- Keep this ratio calmer than the others: fewer arrows, torn notes, and competing accents.

## Text and evidence repair

- Image generation may establish the finished composition and expressive type, but all Chinese, English, brand names, numerals, and UI claims must be inspected at 100%.
- When a narrow deterministic repair is required, preserve the generated composition and replace only the faulty text/UI region. Record the repaired wording and font choice.
- Do not blank, blur, mask, crop, or rewrite visible source information for privacy unless the user explicitly requests concealment.
- Never add a success state, price, guarantee, partnership, discount, compatibility claim, or official affiliation that the video does not prove.

## Acceptance

Check the three independent full-size PNGs and three separate phone-size thumbnails. Within one second at phone size, each must communicate the topic and object. A contact sheet is supplementary only.

Reject any output whose visual system drifts from the fixed paper-collage palette and hierarchy, whose three ratios are mechanical crops, or whose content cannot be traced to the current video.
