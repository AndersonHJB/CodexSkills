# Story Architecture and Publishing Copy

## 1. Select One Post Angle

Summarize the conversation in one sentence:

```text
For [audience], this real conversation shows [problem] and how to approach [useful next step].
```

If the sentence contains two unrelated promises, split the material into separate posts. Prefer reader value over a chronological dump.

Useful angles include:

- How a real need becomes a learning or service plan.
- Questions to ask before choosing a tutor or provider.
- A practical setup, schedule, or preparation checklist.
- Common misconceptions revealed in a consultation.
- How scope, constraints, and next steps were clarified.

## 2. Choose Slide Count by Evidence

Use 6-8 slides for one compact exchange, 9-12 for a conversation with several decisions, and 13-14 only when every slide adds a distinct point.

Recommended sequence:

1. Cover: audience + concrete topic + hook.
2. Context: who needed what, without revealing identity.
3. Friction: the key uncertainty or misconception.
4. Diagnosis: what had to be clarified first.
5. Method: the plan, checklist, or framework.
6. Evidence: relevant chat excerpts or attachments.
7. Decision: the agreed next step, stated conservatively.
8. Takeaway: a reusable lesson for the reader.
9. CTA: optional soft prompt, merged into the last slide when possible.

Do not use one screenshot per slide mechanically. Combine adjacent screenshots when the reader needs both to understand one point; omit low-value screenshots.

## 3. Write Slide Copy

Treat each slide as one claim.

- Eyebrow: 4-10 Chinese characters.
- Headline: ideally 8-18 Chinese characters, no more than two lines.
- Subtitle: one supporting sentence, ideally under 28 Chinese characters.
- Callout title: 4-10 Chinese characters.
- Callout body: one or two short sentences, usually under 45 Chinese characters.
- Footer takeaway: one concrete conclusion, not a slogan.

Use the screenshot to prove the point and annotations to explain why it matters. Do not repeat long visible chat text in a callout.

Prefer specific headings:

```text
先看需求，再定学习路径
基础没确认，课程越排越乱
一周上几次，要看课后练习量
资料先到位，沟通成本会更低
```

Avoid vague headings:

```text
干货满满
一定要看
成功案例
太厉害了
```

## 4. Keep Claims Inside the Evidence

Allowed:

- `对方提到之前没有系统学过 Python。`
- `沟通后先确定了基础、课程范围和上课频率。`
- `这套规划适合有类似课程需求的人参考。`

Not allowed without direct evidence:

- `学生非常满意。`
- `已经成功提分。`
- `这套方法保证学会。`
- `报名后一定能找到工作。`

When the post includes a real transaction, use neutral wording such as `确认课时与下一步` rather than `成功成交` unless that framing is explicitly requested and appropriate.

## 5. Title Package

Provide five options and mark one recommended title. Mix intent rather than producing five cosmetic variants:

1. Search intent: topic + audience + task.
2. Pain point: a concrete mistake or uncertainty.
3. Process: from question to plan.
4. Checklist: what to confirm before starting.
5. Story: a real consultation or communication record.

Keep titles concise enough to scan on mobile, usually within about 20 Chinese characters when possible. Include the core search term naturally. Do not stack punctuation or claim guaranteed results.

## 6. Unified Caption

Write one caption for the whole carousel:

```text
[A concrete question, situation, or pain point]

[What the person needed and why the problem was not solved by a generic answer]

这组图按沟通过程整理了：
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

[The reusable method or checklist readers can apply]

[Soft CTA or question]
```

Use short paragraphs. State that identities were anonymized when real chat records appear. Do not imply that the published screenshots are a complete transcript; say they were selected and organized when appropriate.

## 7. CTA

Match the CTA to the post:

- Educational: ask readers to save the checklist or comment with their situation.
- Service: invite a DM with one useful keyword.
- Case study: ask which step readers find hardest.
- No conversion goal: end with a concrete takeaway and no CTA.

Do not expose personal contact information, embed a QR code, or invent urgency.

## 8. Hashtags

Provide 8-15 tags. Build a relevant mix:

- 2-4 core topic tags.
- 2-4 audience tags.
- 2-4 scenario or problem tags.
- 2-4 long-tail search tags.
- At most one brand tag when appropriate.

Prefer precise tags such as `#Python科学计算` or `#留学生课程辅导` over broad filler such as `#成长` or `#日常`.

## 9. Publishing File Structure

Use these headings in `小红书发布文案.md`:

```markdown
# 小红书发布文案

## 推荐标题
## 备选标题
## 整帖统一发布文案
## 图片顺序说明
## 互动引导
## 标签组合
## 推荐发布标签
## 隐私说明
```

Make the numbered image guide match filenames exactly. If slides change, update the guide before delivery.
