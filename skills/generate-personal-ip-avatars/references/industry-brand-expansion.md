# Industry full-body and official VI theme expansion

Read this file whenever `industry_full_body_pack` or `brand_theme_series` is enabled.

## Industry full-body pack

Generate `industry_count` standalone square front-facing head-to-toe originals. The default eight cover distinct fields rather than job-title synonyms:

1. digital technology / AI engineering
2. education / teaching
3. architecture / spatial or product design
4. health science / medical research
5. business, law, or consulting
6. laboratory science / engineering research
7. photography, media, or content production
8. environment, urban planning, or outdoor sustainability

Adapt fields when the user names preferred professions. For every image define:

- one occupation-readable outfit silhouette or layer system
- one safe signature prop held without covering the body
- two to four abstract industry doodles or decorative shapes
- a different pose, footwear treatment, and accessory family
- the same identity DNA, source-supported hairstyle, age range, body proportions, handmade medium, and white cutout edge

Do not add protected medical crosses, police or military insignia, government seals, company logos, academic regalia, licensed badges, or text credentials. Do not imply the user actually holds a job, license, degree, or affiliation. Avoid gender, age, ethnicity, class, or personality stereotypes. Treat the results as imaginative industry-facing IP concepts.

## Official brand or institution VI themes

Activate only when the user names an organization or supplies an official VI source. Prefer official HTML, PDF, or downloadable assets over third-party summaries.

1. Record the source URL, document title, date/version, page or section, and official color values.
2. Distinguish official numeric values from sampled or inferred screen approximations.
3. Preserve official color relationships such as primary, white, auxiliary, tint, monochrome, or special-process roles.
4. Generate eight complete series by default. Every series gets the currently enabled action, full-body, angle, and emotion modules.
5. Give each series a new outfit silhouette, footwear, action-prop matrix, decoration vocabulary, and canonical anchor. A tint change alone is not a new series.
6. Use abstract motifs and palette relationships by default. Do not reproduce seals, logos, wordmarks, mascots, uniforms, protected architecture, or branded text unless the user explicitly requests it and use is appropriate.
7. State that the output is an unofficial personal color-inspired concept and does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

## Verified Tsinghua University VI example

Re-check the official source at task time:

- color specification: `https://vi.tsinghua.edu.cn/gk/xxbz/scgf.htm`
- Chinese VI manual: `https://vi.tsinghua.edu.cn/download/VisualIdentity-cn.pdf`

The official 2023 Chinese manual specifies:

- university colors: purple and white
- standard purple: PANTONE 259C; CMYK `65,100,0,20`; RGB `102,8,116`; screen hex `#660874`
- auxiliary rose: PANTONE 7424C; CMYK `10,90,20,0`; RGB `217,51,121`; screen hex `#D93379`
- additional special-process roles: PANTONE 871C gold, PANTONE 877C silver, black, and white
- purple tint scale from 100% to 10%; use deep purple linework on light tints and white linework on deep fields

Do not invent official RGB values for PANTONE 871C or 877C. If a raster illustration needs flat screen approximations, label them as sampled visual approximations in `PROMPTS.md`, not official values.

Build eight distinct Tsinghua-color systems when requested:

1. standard purple and white — academic notebook and archive vocabulary
2. standard purple with auxiliary rose — innovation and creative-studio vocabulary
3. deep 90%/50% purple tints — computation and data vocabulary
4. 70%/30% purple tints — architecture and spatial vocabulary
5. 20%/10% light purple tints with standard-purple line — reading and calm-campus vocabulary
6. standard purple with flat gold-process approximation — heritage and ceremonial craft vocabulary
7. standard purple with flat silver-process approximation — engineering and future-material vocabulary
8. standard purple, black, and white — night library and debate vocabulary

Use these as palette-and-concept directions, not permission to place the Tsinghua seal or name inside generated images.
